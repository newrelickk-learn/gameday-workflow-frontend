# How to submit an expense settlement

This is the procedure for settling costs you actually paid during a trip or for work (flights, hotels, transport, business meals, and so on). It is handled separately from an "expense application" for everyday purchases.

## Steps

1. Open "Applications" from the dashboard and choose "New application".
2. Select the expense-related application type.
3. Enter the title, the amount, and the description.
4. Review the details and submit.

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Title | A short name that shows what is being settled (for example: Overseas trip, flights and hotel) |
| Amount | The total amount you actually paid (JPY) |
| Description | The breakdown (flights, hotel, local transport, and so on) and the trip or event it relates to |

## Approval flow (it depends on the amount)

The number of approval steps depends on the size of the amount.

- **Settlements below a certain amount**: your direct manager's approval is enough.
- **Settlements above that amount** (flights and hotels for an overseas trip tend to land here): the company rules require two steps, your direct manager followed by the accounting department. The settlement completes only after accounting approves it as well.

Because of this, a large settlement can take longer than a small expense application. Even after your manager has approved it, the application stays "Pending" until accounting approves it too. Knowing about these two steps up front makes it much easier to read the progress correctly.

## Things to watch out for

- Summarise the evidence of payment (receipts, invoice contents) in the description; approvals go more smoothly.
- When there are several items, list the amount for each category so the approver can check them easily.
