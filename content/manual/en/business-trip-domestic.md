# How to submit a domestic business trip application

This is the procedure for claiming travel and accommodation costs for domestic trips: visiting another office, visiting a customer, attending an internal event, and so on.

## Steps

1. Open "Applications" from the dashboard and choose "New application".
2. Select "Business trip" as the application type.
3. Enter the title, the departure city, the arrival city, the start date, the end date, and the description. The number of days is calculated from the two dates.
4. Once you pick both cities, the estimated travel cost is calculated automatically and filled into the amount field.
5. Review the details and submit.

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Title | A short name that shows the purpose of the trip (for example: Osaka office, regular meeting) |
| From / To | The cities you travel from and to |
| Estimated travel cost | Calculated automatically from the two cities (you cannot type it in) |
| Start date / End date | The first and last day of the trip (use the same day for a day trip) |
| Days | Calculated automatically from the start and end dates |
| Description | The destination, the purpose, who you will visit, and other details (an AI reviewer checks this; see below) |

## About the estimated travel cost

The estimate is produced by an external partner service. That service has been getting slower, and the calculation occasionally fails. When it fails, an error appears in the amount field and you cannot submit the application. In that case, use New Relic to investigate what is happening and deal with it.

## About the AI review of the description

When you submit, an AI reviewer checks the description. If it cannot read the following three things concretely, the application is sent back and the missing points are shown under the description field.

- The purpose of the trip
- Who or where you will visit (office, company, venue, and so on)
- What work you will do there

Entries such as "Going on a trip" or "Please approve" are sent back. If that happens, rewrite the description following the feedback and submit again.

Example:

> Visiting the Sapporo branch to align requirements with their information systems team for next year's order management system migration, over three days.

## Approval

A business trip application is confirmed once your direct manager approves it. You cannot change the contents after approval, so check the dates and the destination carefully beforehand.

## Things to watch out for

- Submit after the schedule is fixed. Applying with tentative dates makes later changes slow.
- If you already know your plans at the destination (who you meet, where you go), write them in the description; approvals go more smoothly.
- Day trips use exactly the same procedure.
