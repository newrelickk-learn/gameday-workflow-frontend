# Frequently asked questions

Common questions about applications and approvals.

## Q. Can I edit an application after submitting it?

A. As a rule, no. If something is wrong, contact the approver, have them reject it, and submit a new application.

## Q. What if I do not know who the approver is?

A. The "Next approver" column in the application list shows who it is waiting on. If nothing is shown, or you get an error, contact general affairs or HR.

## Q. My application has been "Pending" for a long time. What should I do?

A. First check the application list and the approval list to see which step it is on and who it is waiting for. Sending the approver a reminder often helps. If nothing changes, contact the system team.

## Q. Can I resubmit a rejected application?

A. Yes. Read the rejection comment, fix the contents, and submit it as a new application.

## Q. I forgot my login details.

A. Contact the internal account management team. They will guide you through resetting your password.

## Q. Can I apply from a smartphone?

A. Yes, as long as you use a supported browser you can open the dashboard and submit or approve from a phone. Some parts of the screen are harder to read at that width.

## Q. If I close the window while typing, is my input saved?

A. What you type in the application form is temporary until you submit. Closing the window or navigating away loses it, so keep a separate note when you write a long description.
