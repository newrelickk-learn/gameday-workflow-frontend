# How to submit an overseas business trip application

This is the procedure for claiming travel and accommodation costs for visiting an overseas office, attending an overseas conference, or meeting an overseas customer. The steps are the same as for a domestic business trip.

## Steps

1. Open "Applications" from the dashboard and choose "New application".
2. Select "Business trip" as the application type (there is no separate type for domestic and overseas trips).
3. Enter the title, start date, end date, and description. The number of days is calculated from the two dates.
4. Review the details and submit.

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Title | A short name that shows the purpose of the trip (for example: London office, technical conference) |
| Start date / End date | The day you leave and the day you return |
| Days | Calculated automatically from the start and end dates |
| Description | The country and city you visit, the purpose, who travels with you, and other details |

## Approval

An overseas business trip is also confirmed once your direct manager approves it. Preparations such as visas and flight bookings take time, so submit the application with plenty of margin.

## Things to watch out for

- Allow time for preparation (visa, vaccinations, flight and hotel bookings) and submit early.
- The form may show guidance about how many days remain until the departure date.
- Costs incurred during the trip (flights, hotels, and so on) are claimed separately through "Expense settlement". See that manual for details.
