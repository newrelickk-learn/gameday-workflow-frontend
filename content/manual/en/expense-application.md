# How to submit an expense application

This is the procedure for claiming costs you need for day-to-day work: equipment purchases, tool subscriptions, books, and other work-related expenses.

## Steps

1. Open "Applications" from the dashboard and choose "New application".
2. Select "Expense" as the application type.
3. Enter the title, the amount, and the description.
4. Review the details and submit.

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Title | A short name that makes the purpose obvious at a glance (for example: Monitor for development) |
| Amount | The amount you paid or are going to pay (JPY) |
| Description | What it is for and why it is needed, plus quote details, so the approver can make a decision |

## Approval

- Small expenses are approved automatically, or fall below the threshold that requires approval.
- Applications above a certain amount need approval from your direct manager.
- If an approver cannot be determined, the application fails with an error. In that case, contact HR or general affairs.

## Common mistakes

- The title is too generic (for example "Expense") and says nothing about the contents.
- The description is empty or a single word, so the approver cannot judge it.
- The amount is entered with the wrong unit or number of digits.

Before you submit, read the application once from the approver's point of view and check that it says enough.
