# Overview of the approval flow

Applications are processed through different approval flows depending on their type. This page gives you the overall picture.

## How approvals work

1. When an applicant creates an application, the approvers (approval steps) are determined automatically from the contents and the rules.
2. The approver receives an "approval request" notification.
3. The approver approves or rejects it.
4. In a multi-step flow, completing one step sends a notification to the approver of the next step.
5. Once every step is approved, the application status becomes "Approved". If any step rejects it, the status becomes "Rejected".

## Rough number of approval steps

| Application type | Approval steps |
| --- | --- |
| Expense | One step (your direct manager) |
| Business trip (domestic and overseas) | One step (your direct manager) |
| Expense settlement | One or two steps depending on the amount (manager, then accounting) |
| Promotion | The flow defined by the company rules |

## Reading the status

The application list shows the following.

- **Status**: Pending / Approved / Rejected
- **Approval step**: which step it is on, out of how many
- **Next approver**: the person the application is currently waiting on

When you want to check progress, start from the application list.
