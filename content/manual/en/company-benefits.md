# Employee benefits

This page introduces the benefits available to employees. If you have questions about the application procedure, start by asking general affairs.

## Main benefits

### Health and medical support

- Annual health check-up (paid by the company)
- Subsidy for influenza vaccination
- Consultation desk with the occupational physician

### Learning and skill development

- Subsidy for work-related books
- Subsidy for external seminars and conferences
- Corporate access to online learning services

### Ways of working

- Support for setting up a remote work environment (equipment for working from home, and so on)
- Flexible working hours
- Planned allocation of paid leave

### Other

- Subsidy for internal events (social gatherings, team building)
- Congratulatory and condolence payments

## Before you use them

Most benefits require an application and approval in advance. For anything handled through an "expense application", read that manual as well.

The details and scope of each benefit are reviewed from year to year. Check the announcements from general affairs for the latest information.
